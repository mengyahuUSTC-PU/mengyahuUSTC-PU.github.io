### [Trump hosts Dario Amodei for a private White House dinner](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/27/anthropic-trump-dario-amodei-dinner-invite)

Axios reports that Trump is hosting Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for a private White House dinner on Sunday evening, their first one-on-one meeting: Amodei missed last week's state dinner with tech CEOs over a scheduling conflict, Trump then invited him personally, and a larger White House meeting with top AI CEOs is set for Tuesday. Amodei has publicly argued for slowing frontier development, a position the administration has openly dismissed, so the dinner reads as a thaw. The night before, SNL's season premiere spoofed him on Weekend Update, with Jane Wickline playing Amodei as two selves, one media-trained and one that confesses "AI is the devil and I its maker" (see [NBC](https://www.nbc.com/nbc-insider/watch-weekend-update-from-snl-season-52-2026-2027)); in a single weekend, one lab founder landed at the center of both politics and pop culture.

### [Google tests a buy button for Flipkart inside Gemini in India](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/26/google-tests-buying-from-walmart-owned-flipkart-through-gemini-and-ai-mode-in-india/)

Google is testing a "Buy" button on select Flipkart listings inside Gemini and AI Mode in India, leading to a Flipkart-branded checkout for phones, electronics and accessories, with a wider rollout planned for late October ahead of the festive shopping season; there is no formal announcement, only a boilerplate statement to TechCrunch. The contrast is plain: Amazon products appear in the same results without a buy button, and Google holds a minority stake in Flipkart after investing roughly $350 million in 2024. Agent shopping is moving from demos to actual checkout, and which retailer gets a buy button inside the AI interface currently comes down to capital ties, not technology.

### [Simon Willison: 2026 in LLMs (so far)](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/27/2026-in-llms-so-far/)

Simon Willison published the annotated slides and video of his closing keynote at WeAreDevelopers World Congress, a chronological tour of the year: coding agents crossing into day-to-day reliability, personal agents going consumer, and a frontier where the top model changes in weeks. Useful as an index for catching up on the year; the material is largely his own first-hand testing and notes.

**One line for today**: even the lab that argues loudest for slowing down needs a seat at the White House table; in this round of AI politics, absence costs more than any position.
