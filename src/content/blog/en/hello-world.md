This is the first post on this site — and a test post.

This blog will cover three kinds of writing:

1. **In-depth pieces on Responsible AI** — safety, evaluation, and governance, from an engineering and mechanistic point of view;
2. **Takes on major AI events** — judgment calls when something big happens, not news roundups;
3. **A daily AI briefing** — a high signal-to-noise daily digest.

Every post is published in both English and Chinese — use the language switch in the top-right corner.
